## Brand & Style

The design system conveys institutional prestige, academic rigor, and human warmth. Designed primarily for parents, legal guardians, educators, and prospective families, the visual language strikes a deliberate balance between academic authority and genuine approachability.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Modern Educational Warmth**:
- **Clarity & Structure:** Clean compositions, generous white space, and an uncompromising focus on readability reassure parents seeking educational quality and organizational reliability.
- **Warm Institutionalism:** While deep maritime blues establish heritage and trust, vibrant golden accents and soft, open geometries prevent the interface from feeling cold, administrative, or overly bureaucratic.
- **Emotional Resonance:** High-touch digital touchpoints—such as admission applications, campus visit scheduling, and academic calendars—feel supportive, clear, and reassuring rather than transactional.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in authoritative, pedagogical tones balanced by optimistic energy:

- **Primary (`#1B3A6B`):** Deep Navy. Forms the backbone of institutional weight, top-level navigation, primary headings, key branding surfaces, and formal interactive triggers.
- **Secondary (`#F2B705`):** Golden Amber. A radiant, high-energy accent reserved for key calls to action (e.g., "Matrículas Abertas", "Agende uma Visita"), high-priority status indicators, and energetic micro-highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#2E62AB`):** Lighter Cobalt Blue. Used for secondary interaction states, informational badges, links, icon backgrounds, and gentle atmospheric accents.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937`):** Graphite Charcoal. Used for crisp, high-contrast body typography and functional iconography, avoiding the harshness of pure black.
- **Backgrounds & Surfaces:** Section backgrounds alternate between pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and an ultra-soft cool gray (`#F5F7FA`) to structure content clearly without requiring stark dividing lines.

## Typography

The typography pairs **Outfit** (a warm, geometric display typeface sharing the approachable proportions and open round counters of Poppins) with **Inter** for clean, functional body copy.

- **Headlines & Metric Figures (Outfit):** Delivers structural warmth, optimistic geometry, and distinct character. Used across all primary page titles, card headers, and large statistical counters.
- **Body & Functional UI (Inter):** Provides neutral, highly legible micro-typography across long narrative school descriptions, parent communication feeds, tuition tables, and administrative forms.
- **Rhythm & Line Heights:** Headings maintain compact, modern leading for cohesive presence, while body text uses generous line heights (1.5x to 1.6x) to facilitate comfortable scanning for parents reading on mobile screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a responsive **12-column fluid grid** centered within a maximum width of `1280px`.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Editorial sections, grade-level showcases, and statistic rows span standard multi-column subdivisions (3, 4, or 6 columns).
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** 8 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) margins. Dual-column layouts transition naturally to single or twin card arrangements.
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) margins. Multi-column cards collapse to a unified vertical stack or horizontal snap-carousels for testimonials and facility galleries.
- **Alternating Sections:** Major visual sections strictly alternate between `#FFFFFF` and `#F5F7FA`, separated by `4rem` to `6rem` of vertical padding on desktop to create clean, breathable divisions without heavy borders.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft, diffused ambient shadows rather than harsh drop-shadows or heavy outlines:

- **Surface Base (Level 0):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` or cool gray `#F5F7FA`. Un-elevated background planes.
- **Subtle Rest (Level 1 - `shadow-sm`):** `0 1px 3px rgba(27, 58, 107, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(27, 58, 107, 0.04)`. Used for informational cards, input containers, and structural content containers at rest. The shadow is tinted with primary navy instead of pure black for a natural, refined appearance.
- **Elevated / Interactive (Level 2 - `shadow-md`):** `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(27, 58, 107, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(27, 58, 107, 0.04)`. Applied to active navigation bars, dropdown menus, focused modals, and cards on cursor hover.
- **Card Hover Transition:** Interactive cards translate upward by `-3px` while shifting smoothly from Level 1 to Level 2 elevation over `200ms ease-out`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, friendly rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`), projecting safety, empathy, and contemporary design:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Employ `rounded-xl` (16px) and `rounded-2xl` (24px) border radii, creating distinct, welcoming content blocks.
- **Standard Controls:** Input fields, list containers, and standard button elements use `rounded-lg` (8px to 12px) for balanced usability and comfortable touch targets.
- **Badges & Hero CTAs:** Highlight pills (e.g., status tags, "Matrículas Abertas") use full pill radii (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to produce immediate visual contrast against rectangular card layouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Action / Enrollment):** Background `#F2B705`, text `#1B3A6B` (bold), subtle border radius (`rounded-xl`). Hover state transitions to `#DBA604` with a slight upward lift.
- **Institutional Primary:** Background `#1B3A6B`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover state lightens to `#2E62AB`.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #1B3A6B`, text `#1B3A6B`. Hover state fills with a 5% opacity tint of `#1B3A6B`.
- **Sizing & Padding:** Standard height 48px (touch-friendly), horizontal padding `1.5rem` (`space-lg`), font `label-lg`.

### Highlight Pills & Badges
- **Enrollment Pill ("Matrículas Abertas"):** Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), background `#F2B705` with `#1B3A6B` bold text, or soft gold container (`#FEF3C7`) with amber text (`#92400E`). Often paired with an animated pulse dot for seasonal enrollment campaigns.
- **Informational Badges (Grade Levels, Categories):** Soft tint background using 10% `#2E62AB`, text `#1B3A6B`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, font `label-md`.

### Cards
- **Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `rounded-2xl` (24px), padding `1.5rem` to `2rem`, subtle resting shadow (`shadow-sm`).
- **Interactive State:** Lifts `-3px` on desktop hover with an ambient navy shadow expansion (`shadow-md`) and smooth color shifts on internal action links.

### Statistical Counters
- **Presentation:** Centered or vertically stacked metric modules. Number styled in `counter-display` using `#1B3A6B` or `#F2B705`.
- **Accompanying Label:** Styled in `body-sm` or `label-lg` using `#1F2937` with an uppercase tracking offset to highlight metrics like approval rates, educational acreage, or bilingual proficiency.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E5E7EB`, border-radius `rounded-xl`. Active focus transitions border to `#2E62AB` with a 3px ring of `#2E62AB` at 15% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded corners (`4px` for checkboxes, circular for radios), active fill `#1B3A6B` with white checkmark glyph.

### Informational Lists & Event Timelines
- **Lists:** Clean dividers using `#E5E7EB` with `1rem` vertical padding, incorporating icon nodes tinted in `#2E62AB` on circular `#F5F7FA` pill backdrops.
